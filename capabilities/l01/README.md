# Freight Invoice Audit

L01 classifies invoice lines with a registered charge taxonomy and compares each supported amount with the applicable quotation, contract or published tariff. It publishes matched time-based lines for F07 and lists tax lines as excluded from tariff evaluation.

The line view shows billed and expected amounts, the basis and its validity, source citations and calculation traces. Invoice-level checks include duplicate charges and the sum of all lines, including tax.
